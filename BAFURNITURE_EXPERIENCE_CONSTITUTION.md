# BAFurniture Experience Constitution

## Product Promise

BA_Furniture helps businesses, schools, factories, warehouses and project teams choose the right furniture by use case, size, material, color and deployment need.

## UX Principles

1. Search first: users should quickly find product groups and quotation paths.
2. Category clarity: categories must be visible, scannable and route-safe.
3. Conversion clarity: hotline and quote CTA remain available without hiding product discovery.
4. ProductDB safety: UI may read existing bundles but must not write or mutate ProductDB.
5. Portal safety: portal.bafurni.com remains separate and untouched.
6. Visual reliability: unstable images fall back to branded composite visuals.
7. Mobile first recovery: no horizontal overflow, sticky CTA available on small screens.

## Design Direction

- Premium office furniture supplier.
- Bright, clean, practical, high-trust.
- Navy/green, gold accent, white surface.
- Composite category imagery instead of icon-only hero cards.

## Technical Rules

- No force push.
- No history rewrite.
- No ProductDB or Portal edits.
- No fake backend.
- No fake Zalo URL.
- Preserve sitemap, robots, clean routes and product routes.
